# UI Cleanup Instructions

## Problem
The UI has too much going on. Too many widgets, banners, tickers, heatmaps, charts crammed everywhere. It looks cluttered, not clean.

## What to do NEXT SESSION:

### Remove or simplify:
- Remove the scrolling trade ticker marquee - distracting
- Remove the position heatmap - not useful enough for the space it takes
- Remove the "simulation active" indicators - we're using real data now
- Simplify the markets page header - too many stats crammed in
- Remove the AI signals badges on market cards - too noisy

### Keep it clean:
- ONE clear number per card, not multiple stats crammed in
- More whitespace between sections
- Less borders and glassmorphism effects - getting overused
- Fewer colors - stick to green for profit, red for loss, blue for actions, white for text
- The 98-99 strategy page is good - use that as the design benchmark

### Pages should be:
1. Markets - clean grid of real Polymarket markets with prices. That's it.
2. Portfolio - your positions with P&L. Simple table.
3. AI Trades - what the AI is holding and why. Clean.
4. 98-99 Strategy - the scanner. This one is good already.
5. AI Brain - strategy overview and daily report. Not a dashboard of 10 widgets.

### Principle:
If Krish can't understand what he's looking at in 2 seconds, it's too complex. Remove it.
